ABOUT

Music can meet us where words can't.

Welcome to Loading, a private music therapy practice centered around connection, creativity, and individualized care.

Music therapy can provide a meaningful way to explore emotions, build skills, express yourself, and connect with others. Sessions are shaped around each person's unique needs, interests, and goals.

MY APPROACH

There is no one-size-fits-all approach.

Every person brings their own experiences, preferences, strengths, and relationship with music.

Sessions are collaborative and person-centered. Together, we can explore musical experiences that feel meaningful and supportive.

Whether you have years of musical experience or have never played an instrument, you don't need to be a musician to participate in music therapy.
